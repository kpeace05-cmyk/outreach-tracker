Outreach Tracker

A shared team app for managing contact outreach. Let's get it connected to a database first.

Step 1 of 2 — Create free database
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and sign in with Google
  2. Click "Add project", give it any name (e.g. outreach-tracker), disable Google Analytics, click Create
  3. In the left sidebar, click "Build" → "Realtime Database"
  4. Click "Create Database", choose your region, select "Start in test mode", click Enable
  5. Your database URL will appear — it looks like https://your-app-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com
  6. In the left sidebar click the gear icon → "Project settings"
  7. Scroll to "Your apps" → click "</>" (Web) → register the app → copy the firebaseConfig values
Step 2 of 2 — Enter Firebase config

Your config is saved in your browser (localStorage) so you won't need to enter it again.
